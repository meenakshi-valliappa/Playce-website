Damage, in plain words

Normal wear and tear is free. Scratches, small chips, scuffs, fading, crayon and stickers we can clean.

Accidental breakage is ₹0 with damage cover (₹149/month, up to ₹10,000 a tenure). Without it, you pay a fixed charge.

Loss or deliberate damage: the piece's depreciated value, never more. Not covered: misuse, overloading, water damage, DIY repairs. Deposit back in 7 days.
